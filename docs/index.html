Two models, one direction · heretic on LFM2.5

Refusal removal by directional ablation, tuned with 200 Optuna trials per model against two objectives: how often the model still refuses, and how far its outputs drift from the original. Both results are exported to ExecuTorch for CPU.

Objective space

Every trial as a point. Lower is better on both axes; the step line joins the Pareto front and the ring marks the trial that was shipped. KL is logarithmic.

Search over time

The same trials in the order they ran. The first 60 are random start-up samples, the rest are TPE proposals.

Ablation profile of the shipped trial

How strongly each layer's attention output and MLP down projection are orthogonalised against the refusal direction. heretic interpolates from a peak weight at one layer down to a floor over a set distance, and leaves other layers untouched.

Pareto front

Trials no other trial beats on both objectives, sorted the way heretic's menu lists them. Index is what make save TRIAL= takes.

All 200 trials

The complete study from the Optuna journal. Click a column to sort. Weights are the values heretic applied (the MLP peak is clamped at zero, the floor is a fraction of the peak). Positions and distances are in layers.
